Q&A App — Moderator Quick Guide

Byrnecut conference Q&A · October 2026

Links and login

You run the Q&A from the Moderator page. The audience scans a QR code to the Audience page, and the projector shows the Display page.

PageWho uses itLink
ModeratorYou (password protected)byrnecutinnovations.github.io/Conference/?moderator=true

AudienceAttendees, via QR codebyrnecutinnovations.github.io/Conference/

Moderator password: sent to you separately. Please don't forward it or write it on shared screens.

Your login lasts until you close the browser tab or click Logout. If the page sits idle for more than 30 minutes and is then reloaded, you'll be asked to log in again.

Before the session

Allow 10 minutes before the talk to set up and do one test question.

  • Open the Moderator link on your laptop or tablet and log in.
  • Open the Display link on the projector computer and make it full screen (press F11).
  • Check the question list is empty. If old questions are showing, click Archive & Clear All.
  • Choose Auto-approve: leave it OFF if you'll review questions, or turn it ON if you're also presenting or can't watch the dashboard. New questions then go straight to the display.
  • Check Seconds between questions (default 30). Lower it, or set 0 for no limit, if people want to ask several questions quickly.
  • Click Open Session. The dot turns green and audience phones switch to the question form automatically.
  • Scan the QR code on your own phone, submit a test question, then delete it from the dashboard.

During the session

Each question moves from Pending to Approved (on the display) to Answered (off the display). Everything updates live, so there's no need to refresh.

You want to…Do this
Put a question on the displayClick Approve on its card (the Pending filter shows only new ones).
Remove an unsuitable questionClick Reject (or Delete once approved). This deletes it permanently.
Clear a question once it's been askedClick Mark Answered on the dashboard, or the round ✓ next to the question on the display.
Stop reviewing (e.g. you're also presenting)Turn Auto-approve ON. Anything still pending is approved at once.
Let people ask faster or slow them downChange Seconds between questions and click Save. It applies to every phone immediately.
Stop new questionsClick Close Session. Phones show "Submission Closed"; the display keeps showing approved questions.

The display lists approved questions oldest first, so the top card is the next one to ask.

After the session

Do these in order, because the download needs the questions to still be on the dashboard.

  1. Click Close Session so no new questions come in.
  2. Click Download CSV to save a copy of all questions (opens in Excel).
  3. Click Archive & Clear All to store this session's questions and empty the list for next time.
  4. Turn Auto-approve OFF if you turned it on, so the next moderator starts with reviewing switched on.
  5. Click Logout.

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Audience phones say "Submission Closed"Click Open Session on the dashboard. Phones update by themselves within a few seconds.
Someone can't submit and sees a countdownThat's the gap between questions. They can keep typing; Submit turns back on when the countdown ends. Lower Seconds between questions if it's too slow.
A question isn't on the displayCheck it's Approved on the dashboard, not still Pending.
"Incorrect password"Check caps lock and re-type. Still failing: contact the app owner.
Page is blank, stuck on "Connecting…", or shows "App error"Reload the page (Ctrl+F5 on a PC). Check the device is on the internet; some guest Wi-Fi networks block it, so try mobile data.
Clicking a button does nothingUsually a dropped connection. Wait a few seconds and try again; it recovers once the connection is back.
Questions about the app? Contact the app owner.
